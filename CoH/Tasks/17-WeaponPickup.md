# 17 Weapon pickup (stretch)

**Branch** `coh/weapon-pickup` · **Needs** 09 · **Slice** `CoH/Features/WeaponPickup/` · **Flag** `WeaponPickup`

## Goal

Battlefields leave useful wreckage behind.

## Behavior

- A squad member carrying a special weapon (rocket launcher, machine gun) drops it when killed.
- Any squad can pick it up, replacing one member's weapon.
- Dropped weapons vanish after a while.

## Done

Weapons can be dropped and picked up in game.
