# Ideas

## Territory cut-off

From Company of Heroes: a capture point only pays its owner while it is linked to the owner's base through a chain of the owner's own points. Taking one point in the middle of a chain cuts off every point behind it, so raids deep into enemy ground matter more than the point's own income.

- Needs links between points on every map. These could be worked out automatically (each point links to its nearest neighbours) or placed by hand per map.
- Set aside (2026-10-12): in a modern setting supply lines matter less than they did in the Second World War, so this may not fit. Worth trying once capture points work.
