# 14 Trenches (later)

**Branch** `coh/trenches` · **Needs** 09 · **Slice** `CoH/Features/Trenches/` · **Flag** `Trenches`

An idea for later (the user, 2026-10-19). It replaces the cancelled Cover system (05).

## Goal

There is no cover system: squads fight squads, as in Axis & Allies (2004).
Positional play comes from **trenches that builders dig and squads garrison**.

## Behavior (to be designed with the user)

- Builders (Dozer, Worker, faction engineers) dig trenches in owned or contested sectors. They are cheap and quick, and can be destroyed.
- A trench is a garrison that holds one or more squads, using the engine's garrison containers, which count squads as one through Squads (09).
  - Squads inside fire out and take less damage.
  - Explosives, flamers and toxins (GLA) are the counters.
- Open questions:
  - Is a trench low and open, so tanks drive over it or crush it?
  - Can vehicles hull-down in one?
  - Should other field defenses, such as barbed wire and tank traps, come back too?
- The AI digs and holds trenches in the sectors it defends.
