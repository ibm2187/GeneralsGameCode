# 14 Field defenses

**Branch** `coh/field-defenses` · **Needs** 05 · **Slice** `CoH/Features/FieldDefenses/` · **Flag** `FieldDefenses`

## Goal

Hold ground anywhere, not only at the base.

## Behavior

- Builders (Dozer, Worker, faction engineers) build these in any owned or contested sector:
  - sandbags (heavy cover)
  - barbed wire (blocks infantry)
  - tank traps (block vehicles)
- They are cheap, quick to build and can be destroyed.
- The AI fortifies the sectors it holds.

## Done

The defenses can be built, give cover through Cover, and block movement as intended.
